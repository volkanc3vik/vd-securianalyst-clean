Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026 · Version 1.0

At VD SecuriAnalyst, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

1. Data We Collect

The following data may be collected through the Platform:

Data You Provide Directly

  • Contact data: Name, e-mail (contact form, waiting list).
  • Message content: Texts you send via the contact form.

Automatically Collected Data

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system.
  • Usage data: Pages viewed, duration of visit (only if cookie consent is given).
  • Device data: Screen resolution, language preference.

2. Purposes of Data Use

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve Platform services.
  • To respond to your contact requests.
  • To manage waiting-list applications.
  • To produce anonymous statistics (number of visitors, popular pages, etc.).
  • To fulfill legal obligations.
  • To detect security threats.

3. Data Retention Periods

  • Contact data: Up to 2 years after your request is completed.
  • Waiting list: Until the launch date + 6 months.
  • Technical logs: Up to 30 days.
  • Cookie data: The periods specified in the cookie policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties, nor do we share it for marketing purposes.

Data is shared only in the following cases:

  • Service providers: Infrastructure providers such as Vercel (hosting) and Supabase (database), under DPA agreements.
  • Legal obligation: Court orders or legal requests.
  • Security: Detection of attacks or fraud.
  • With your permission: Where you have given explicit consent.

5. Data Security

The measures we take to protect your data include:

  • Encrypted communication over HTTPS.
  • Vercel infrastructure security standards.
  • Data access control via Supabase RLS (Row Level Security).
  • Storage of sensitive data (e.g. IP) in hashed form.
  • Keeping secrets such as bot tokens and API keys out of the frontend.

However, no internet service is 100% secure; even with the strictest measures, the risk of a data breach cannot be entirely eliminated.

6. Cookies

The Platform uses cookies. For detailed information, please review our Cookie Policy page.

7. User Rights

You have the following rights over your personal data:

  • To learn which of your data is being processed.
  • To question the purpose of processing and how the data is used.
  • To request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • To request the deletion of data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • To object to data processing.
  • To request data portability.

To exercise these rights: privacy@vd-securianalyst.com

Detailed KVKK rights for users residing in Türkiye: KVKK Information Notice

8. Children

The Platform is not suitable for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18. If you become aware that your child has provided data, please contact us.

9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (Vercel, Supabase) may process data in different countries. These transfers are protected by GDPR- and KVKK-compliant Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC).

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be announced on the Platform. We recommend reviewing the policy regularly.

11. Contact

For questions about the privacy policy: privacy@vd-securianalyst.com

For detailed contact options, you can visit our Contact page.
