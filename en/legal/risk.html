Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 30 May 2026 · Version 1.0

⚠ Important Warning

Cryptocurrency transactions involve high risk. Remember that you face the risk of losing the entire amount you invest. Please only trade with amounts you can afford to lose.

1. General Risk Statement

Cryptocurrency markets have dynamics that differ from traditional financial markets. Before trading in these markets, you must carefully read and understand the risk factors below.

2. Risk of Total Loss

The most significant risk in cryptocurrency markets is the possibility of losing the entire amount invested. This risk may materialize due to:

  • The coin's value dropping to zero (especially for altcoins)
  • Exchange bankruptcy or a hack attack
  • Wallet loss or forgotten private keys
  • Rug pulls (a project owner taking the funds and disappearing)
  • Smart contract bugs

Only trade with an amount you can afford to lose.

3. Extreme Volatility

Cryptocurrency prices are extremely volatile. Historically:

  • Losses of 30-50% can occur within a single day.
  • Prices can double or halve within hours.
  • Drops of 90%+ are not rare in low-liquidity coins.
  • Leveraged positions carry the risk of instant liquidation.

4. Liquidity Risk

Some cryptocurrencies may not have sufficient trading volume. This can cause:

  • Being unable to buy or sell at the price you want,
  • Significant slippage when exiting large positions,
  • Amplified losses in situations requiring an urgent exit.

5. Regulatory Risk

Cryptocurrency markets are not yet fully regulated. In Türkiye and other countries:

  • New tax obligations may be introduced.
  • Certain coins or exchanges may be banned.
  • User obligations (KYC, reporting) may become stricter.
  • In Türkiye, cryptocurrencies currently cannot be used as a "means of payment".

Such changes may negatively affect your portfolio.

6. Technical Risks

Exchange Risks

  • Exchange hack attacks (billions of dollars have been stolen in the past).
  • Exchange bankruptcy or withdrawal freezes.
  • System failures, sudden price gaps, "flash crash" events.

Wallet Risks

  • Loss of private keys = irreversible loss of funds.
  • Phishing attacks, wallet takeover via malware.
  • Transfers to a wrong address = unrecoverable loss.

Smart Contract Risks

  • Software bugs in DeFi protocols.
  • Unaudited contracts.
  • Exploits and flash-loan attacks.

7. Taxation

The taxation of gains from cryptocurrency transactions is a constantly changing area. While there is currently no clarity on the tax obligations of crypto gains in Türkiye, taxation in the future is likely. Tax obligations rest entirely with the user. Please consult a tax advisor.

8. Psychological Risks

By the nature of crypto markets:

  • FOMO (Fear of Missing Out): Hasty decisions during rapid rallies.
  • Panic selling: Emotional decisions during declines.
  • Addiction: Constant chart-watching, disrupted sleep patterns.
  • Excessive leverage: The desire to "get rich quickly".
  • Herd mentality: Getting caught up in "pumps" seen on social media.

These psychological triggers can drive even the most experienced investors into harmful decisions.

9. Market Manipulation

Crypto markets are more open to manipulation than traditional markets. Common manipulation methods:

  • Pump & Dump: Coordinated pumping and selling of low-volume coins.
  • Wash trading: Creating fake volume.
  • Spoofing: Misleading the market with fake orders.
  • Fake news: Influencing prices through false news on social media.
  • Whale movements: Large wallet holders misleading small investors.

10. Algorithmic System Risks

Algorithmic analysis platforms like VD SecuriAnalyst also carry their own risks:

  • Algorithms inherently carry a margin of error.
  • Models trained on historical data may fail to anticipate future "black swan" events.
  • Manipulation in data sources may mislead the algorithms.
  • System outages may restrict access to analyses at critical moments.

Do not use algorithmic outputs as a standalone decision mechanism.

11. Social Media and Influencer Risk

"Advice"-style content shared on social media:

  • Is usually intended as advertising or promotion.
  • Influencers may buy before a pump and sell during it ("rug").
  • They may receive undisclosed payments from coin developers.
  • Advice from persons not authorized by the relevant regulator may lead to legal problems.

12. Leverage Risk

⚠ Very High Risk

Futures and leveraged trades involve exponentially increasing risk. At 10x leverage, a 10% move against you = full closure of the position (liquidation). Beginners should stay away from leverage.

13. Our Recommendations

Before trading in the crypto market:

  • Get educated: Do serious research on technical analysis, fundamental analysis, and market dynamics.
  • Start small: Gain experience with symbolic amounts in your first trades.
  • Diversify: Avoid putting your entire portfolio into a single coin.
  • Be disciplined: Plan ahead; do not decide based on emotions.
  • Use stops: Set your loss limit in advance.
  • Consult a professional: For large amounts, see a financial advisor authorized by the relevant regulator.
  • Only trade with an amount you can afford to lose.

14. Help Resources

If you believe you are experiencing crypto addiction or have developed a habit of uncontrolled trading, please seek help from a psychologist or addiction specialist. In Türkiye, the Yeşilay (Green Crescent) and similar organizations provide support.
