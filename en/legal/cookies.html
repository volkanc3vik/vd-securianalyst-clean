Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026 · Version 1.0

This Cookie Policy explains which cookies we use on the VD SecuriAnalyst platform, why we use them, and how you can manage them.

1. What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored in your browser when you visit a website. Cookies make it possible to:

  • Remember your site preferences (language, theme, etc.).
  • Manage sessions.
  • Collect site usage statistics.
  • Apply security measures.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

A) Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the platform's core functions and cannot be disabled. They do not require consent.

Examples:

  • Session management
  • Security (CSRF protection)
  • Terms of Use acceptance (welcome modal)
  • Remembering your cookie preference

B) Performance / Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect anonymous data to understand how visitors use the platform. They are not used without your consent.

  • Which pages are visited
  • Duration of the visit
  • Browser and device information (anonymous)
  • Referral sources (e.g. arriving from Telegram)

C) Functionality Cookies

Used to remember your preferences:

  • Selected symbol
  • Theme preference (in the future)
  • Language preference (in the future)

D) Marketing / Third-Party Cookies

VD SecuriAnalyst does not currently use any third-party marketing cookies. If such use occurs in the future, this policy will be updated and your explicit consent will be obtained.

3. Cookie List

Cookies and localStorage keys currently used on the platform:

Key Type Purpose Duration
vd_cookie_consent Necessary Remembers your cookie preference 1 year
vd_welcome_seen Necessary Avoids showing the first-use modal again 6 months
vd_active_symbol Functionality Remembers the last selected coin 30 days
vd_referrer Analytics Records anonymously which source you arrived from 30 days

Note: The platform currently uses the more secure localStorage and sessionStorage rather than HTTP cookies.

4. Cookie Consent

On your first visit to the platform you will see a banner asking for your cookie preference. You have three options:

  • Accept All: Enables all cookie categories.
  • Necessary Only: Enables only strictly necessary cookies.
  • Details: Redirects to this page.

5. Changing Your Cookie Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time:

Via Your Browser

All modern browsers offer cookie management:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Via the Platform

You can readjust your preferences via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.

Clearing localStorage

You can also clear it from the browser console:

localStorage.removeItem('vd_cookie_consent');
localStorage.removeItem('vd_welcome_seen');
location.reload();

6. Third-Party Services

The platform uses the following third-party services:

  • Vercel (hosting infrastructure) — may use its own necessary cookies.
  • Supabase (database) — only active during form submissions.
  • Binance API (price data) — does not use cookies, API calls only.
  • CoinGlass API (market data) — does not use cookies.

We recommend reviewing the cookie policies of these services.

7. Effects of Rejecting Cookies

If you reject cookies:

  • The platform's core functions continue to work.
  • Your preferences may not be remembered (the welcome modal may be shown on every visit).
  • No statistics are collected (no loss for you, just a lost improvement opportunity for us).

9. Use of Marketing Cookies
