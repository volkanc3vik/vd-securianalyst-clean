Last updated: 30 May 2026 · Version 1.0

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), we would like to inform you about the following matters regarding the processing of your personal data.

1. Data Controller

Data Controller / Platform Operator: Volkan Çevik
Address: The full address may be shared upon request within the scope of official applications and legal proceedings.
E-mail: privacy@vd-securianalyst.com
Web: https://vd-securianalyst.com

2. Categories of Personal Data Processed

The following categories of personal data are processed through the Platform:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name (if you fill in the contact form).
  • Contact Data: E-mail address.
  • Transaction Security Data: IP address, session information.
  • Legal Transaction Data: Data retained for the purpose of responding to legal requests.
  • Customer Transaction Data: Request and complaint information.
  • Marketing Data: Anonymous usage statistics via cookies (with your consent).

3. Purposes of Processing Personal Data

Your personal data is processed in accordance with the principles set out in Article 4 of the KVKK and under the conditions specified in Articles 5 and 6, for the following purposes:

  • Provision and operation of Platform services.
  • Responding to contact requests.
  • Management of waiting-list registrations.
  • Improvement of service quality.
  • Detection of security threats.
  • Fulfillment of legal obligations.
  • Protection of the data controller's legitimate interests, provided that the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject are not harmed.

4. Legal Basis

Your personal data is processed pursuant to Article 5/2 of the KVKK on the following legal grounds:

  • 5/2-c: Being directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract.
  • 5/2-ç: Fulfillment of a legal obligation.
  • 5/2-f: The legitimate interests of the data controller, provided that the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject are not harmed.

For marketing cookies, your explicit consent (5/1-a) is additionally obtained.

5. Transfer of Personal Data

Your personal data may be shared with the following parties in accordance with the conditions set out in Articles 8 and 9 of the KVKK:

  • Service providers: Vercel (hosting — USA), Supabase (database — EU).
  • Legal authorities: Court orders, public prosecutors, or legal requests by authorized institutions.
  • Business partners: Business partners essential for delivering the service (under DPA agreements).

Transfers abroad are carried out via standard contractual clauses within the scope of Article 9 of the KVKK.

6. Method of Collecting Personal Data

Your personal data is collected:

  • Directly from you via the contact form,
  • During waiting-list applications,
  • Automatically through cookies and similar technologies,
  • Via technical logs during your use of the Platform.

7. KVKK Article 11 — Rights of the Data Subject

Pursuant to Article 11 of the KVKK, as a data subject you have the following rights:

  1. To learn whether your personal data is being processed.
  2. To request information if your personal data has been processed.
  3. To learn the purpose of processing your personal data and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose.
  4. To know the third parties, domestically or abroad, to whom your personal data has been transferred.
  5. To request the correction of your personal data if it has been processed incompletely or inaccurately.
  6. To request the deletion or destruction of your personal data within the conditions set out in Article 7 of the KVKK.
  7. To request that the actions taken under items (5) and (6) be notified to the third parties to whom your personal data has been transferred.
  8. To object to an outcome unfavorable to you arising from the analysis of your processed data exclusively through automated systems.
  9. To request compensation for damages in case you suffer harm due to the unlawful processing of your personal data.
  10. If you have given explicit consent to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw that consent.
  11. To object to automated decision-making processes.

8. How to Apply

You may submit your applications under the KVKK to privacy@vd-securianalyst.com. To exercise the rights listed above:

  • E-mail: privacy@vd-securianalyst.com
  • Postal mail: The full address is shared upon request within official application processes.

Your application must include the following information:

  • First name, last name, and signature (for written applications).
  • Turkish ID number for citizens of the Republic of Türkiye.
  • Nationality and passport number for foreign nationals.
  • Residential or workplace address for notification purposes.
  • E-mail address and telephone number for notification, if available.
  • The subject of the request.

The Data Controller will conclude the application free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If the process additionally requires a cost, the fee set out in the tariff specified in the Regulation on the Data Controllers' Registry may be charged.

9. Data Security Measures

The technical and administrative measures we take for the security of your personal data include:

  • HTTPS encrypted communication.
  • Database access controls (RLS).
  • Server-side storage of API keys and tokens.
  • Authorized access management (admin key, role-based access).
  • Regular security reviews.

10. Complaint to the KVKK Authority

If you do not receive a satisfactory response to your application above, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).
